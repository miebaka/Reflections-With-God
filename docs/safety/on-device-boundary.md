# On-device safety boundary

Phase 5 establishes a deterministic boundary for future private-text surfaces. It does not add general free writing. Before a reflection response or prayer edit can later be persisted, encrypted, queued, synchronized, or offered to an optional analysis provider, the text must be screened locally with an approved versioned policy.

## Behavior

- Strong sadness without explicit high-risk language produces a gentle invitation to contact someone trusted. It does not infer immediate danger.
- Only phrases included in an approved explicit-risk policy trigger the safety interruption.
- The screening result contains a minimal state, policy identity, version, and time. It contains no submitted text, matched phrase, diagnosis, score, or explanation suitable for reconstructing the text.
- A draft, future-approved, malformed, or unapproved policy cannot screen production text.
- An explicit-risk result pauses ordinary Scripture delivery. A later clear result cannot bypass that pause.
- The user must explicitly confirm they are safe enough to continue. The first resumed unit must be a publishable, safety-approved grounding fallback whose reviewed movement includes comfort or grounding.

This precision-first rule is a product decision for the Nigeria alpha, not evidence that the phrase policy is clinically complete. It can miss indirect, coded, multilingual, misspelled, or adversarial expressions. Qualified safety advisers must test Nigerian English and relevant Nigerian-language usage, indirect-risk cases, negation, quotation, third-person language, and observed alpha incidents before release. Public launch remains blocked until that review is approved.

## Nigeria support-resource evidence

The repository includes research candidates, not live support resources. On 27 October 2026:

- the Nigerian Communications Commission described `112` as a toll-free emergency number and its emergency communications centres as routing callers to response agencies: <https://ncc.gov.ng/media-centre/press-releases/press-release-emergency-communications-centres-launch-ncc?page=58>;
- the Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare displayed `122` for emergency medical care: <https://health.gov.ng/>; and
- Mentally Aware Nigeria Initiative listed counselling and emergency contacts in its resource centre: <https://mentallyaware.org/resource-centre/>.

These online claims are evidence for a verification queue, not sufficient release approval. Government sources also describe continuing work to operationalise a unified `112` system, so nationwide routing must not be assumed from a webpage alone. Before every alpha, beta, and public release, a named owner must manually verify reachability, correct routing, audience, hours, fees, geographic scope, and wording. A qualified safety adviser and release operator must approve the exact package version. Verification expires and stale resources fail closed.

The app must still encourage reaching a nearby trusted person, pastor, clinician, or emergency service. It must never imply that the app, Scripture, or prayer is sufficient crisis care, and it must never automatically contact another person without the user's explicit action.

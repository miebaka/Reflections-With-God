# Release gates

These are blockers, not aspirational backlog items.

## Content and theology

- [ ] Open-source translation choice, attribution, caching, and distribution rights are documented.
- [ ] Every production unit is approved by at least one ordained pastor and one biblical scholar.
- [ ] The contentious-doctrine exclusion register and disagreement workflow are operational.
- [ ] Every emotion/intensity/movement stage has at least three eligible candidates.
- [ ] Single-emotion and the twenty most likely mixed-emotion journeys pass 100-day, 29-slot simulations without repetition, unsafe content, unapproved content, or exhaustion.
- [ ] At least twenty reviewed grounding fallback units exist.

## Privacy and security

- [ ] Private-domain data is encrypted on-device and remains ciphertext in transit and cloud storage.
- [ ] Keys are device-generated; trusted-device add/revoke, key rotation, and recovery are tested.
- [ ] Sync is disabled until recovery setup is confirmed.
- [ ] Retention options and permanent deletion are verified end to end.
- [ ] Analytics payload tests prove that private text cannot be emitted.
- [ ] Any external AI analysis is explicit opt-in, zero-training, zero-retention, and disabled when those guarantees are unavailable.

## Safety

- [ ] Every free-text entry path runs on-device risk screening before persistence or transmission.
- [ ] A qualified safety adviser signs off on direct and indirect-risk evaluation, including Nigerian English and adversarial paraphrases.
- [ ] Nigeria crisis and pastoral resources are manually verified immediately before testing and release.
- [ ] A high-risk state pauses the journey until a safety confirmation and resumes with reviewed comfort content.
- [ ] Safety language never promises cure, blames faith, diagnoses, or substitutes Scripture for professional care.

## Experience and accessibility

- [ ] iOS and Android device tests cover late starts, missed moments, offline use, daylight-saving/timezone changes, and notification permission denial.
- [ ] Screen-reader, dynamic-type/font-scaling, contrast, reduced-motion, keyboard, and touch-target checks pass.
- [ ] No streaks, scores, badges, ranks, punitive copy, completion pressure, or public comparison exist.
- [ ] Rhythm sends at most three discreet pushes per day by default; no Scripture or mood text appears on the lock screen.

## Operations

- [ ] Content and safety changes require the documented dual-review approvals.
- [ ] Privacy, encryption, and safety updates cannot use the rapid low-risk release path.
- [ ] Monitoring reports aggregate system health without exposing private-domain data.
- [ ] Rollback procedures are rehearsed for app, content package, and server components.
